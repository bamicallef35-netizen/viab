viab

stigmergic accretion under a viability constraint / mechanism after TERMES, Werfel, Petersen & Nagpal, 2014 / name and premise after R&Sie(n), I've heard about… (2005) and Une architecture des humeurs (2010) / source / benmicallef

Many simple agents walk the surface of a growing structure on a 2-D lattice. Each perceives only a small patch around itself, deposits at most one cell per step if the local rules allow, then steps to a neighbouring surface cell. No agent reads another agent's state. The only thing they share is the structure they are building, so coordination happens through the material — stigmergy, the mechanism of the TERMES robots.

A deposit is allowed only where it stays viable: attached to existing material, supported (on the ground, on the cell below, or cantilevered no further than a fixed limit), and without sealing a void. Depositing only ever adds material, and every one of those conditions is monotonic under adding material, so a cell that is supported when placed stays supported forever. A synthetic humours signal leans the growth — stress toward dense enclosure, calm toward open reach — and never overrides the rule.

what it is not

Not a robot, not a building, not a reproduction. TERMES compiles a target structure into local rules offline; this has no target at all. R&Sie(n)'s Viab was imagined as a single secreting machine, not a swarm; the name is borrowed with attribution. Support here is a combinatorial invariant, not statics.
